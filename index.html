Built for park days

The easy stay before and after Shanghai LEGOLAND.

Big Bear Little Bear is a family-run homestay in Shanghai's Jinshan District, just 1.14 km from Shanghai LEGOLAND Resort. It is made for parents who want less logistics, happier children, and a calmer night after a full day of rides, shows and walking.

Opened in 2025 with seven uniquely themed rooms, the homestay offers a personal, child-focused experience that standard hotels cannot match. Our family hosts are known for warm, responsive service — guests repeatedly mention our hospitality in their reviews.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A Chinese-style breakfast is included for all guests, featuring local favorites like Babi steamed buns (巴比馒头). It is simple, convenient, and suitable for both adults and children before a park day.

How far is it from Shanghai LEGOLAND?

The homestay is approximately 1.14 km from Shanghai LEGOLAND Resort, which is about a 10-minute walk. Private car transfers to the park can also be arranged with the host.

Can the host pick us up from Jinshan North Railway Station?

Yes. Private transfer service to and from Jinshan North Railway Station can be arranged with the host. Transfers to Shanghai LEGOLAND are also available.

What facilities are best for children?

Children love the indoor slide, building blocks, toys, themed rooms (Mario, Minecraft, Doraemon, Minions, Peppa Pig, One Piece, Tetris), and shared play areas. Selected rooms also include projectors and smart toilets.

How many rooms does the homestay have?

The homestay has 7 uniquely themed rooms across three floors: Mario Game Twin Room (60 sqm, 3F), Tetris Slide Twin Room (25 sqm, 2F), One Piece Queen Room (25 sqm, 1F), Doraemon Queen Room (20 sqm, 2F), Minions Queen Room (20 sqm, 2F), Peppa Pig Queen Room (20 sqm, 1F), and Minecraft Tatami Room (20 sqm, 2F).

Where should international guests book?

The property is listed on Trip.com with a rating of 9.6/10 from 68 guest reviews. Use the booking button or any room card to check availability, prices, and dates.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 12:00. The front desk is available 24/7, and the host will greet you upon arrival. Self check-in is also available — contact the property for the access code.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed at the homestay.
